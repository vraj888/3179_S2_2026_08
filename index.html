Sandstone is the dominant medium of fossil preservation.

Where fossils are found

Fossil sites by period, and how the latitude where a dinosaur lived compares with where its fossil is found today.

Continental drift helps explain why fossil sites cluster in the Northern Hemisphere's mid-latitudes today. Reconstructing the continents' positions when these organisms lived reveals that they were more spread out across the globe.

05

Marine Fossils

Some of the marine fossils have been discovered on mountain tops.

Annotations

Fossils found high in mountain ranges suggest these regions were once submerged beneath ancient seas. Over millions of years, tectonic uplift raised marine sediments and the fossils preserved within them, leaving a striking record of past oceans in places that are now thousands of meters above sea-level.

06

Australia's fossil hotspots

Fossil discoveries across Australia, categorised by state.

Annotations

Victoria leads in the total number of fossil discoveries, while Queensland stands out for the variety of its finds across numerous discovery sites. This contrast shows that a region with the most fossils is not necessarily the region with the broadest range of discoveries.
